## Icons & illustrations

All icons are hand-coded inline SVG (no icon font/library dependency). All product photography, material swatches, hero visuals, blog cover art, team portraits and avatars in `assets/images/` are original flat-vector SVG illustrations created for this template — no third-party stock photography is used, so nothing here requires an external license.

Team portraits, testimonial avatars, the 404 illustration and the finish swatches remain original SVG artwork (see below).
