About Printora Labels

Our Story

Started in a Garage. Now Printing 10M+ Labels a Year.

Printora Labels started in 2014 when our founder, a packaging designer, got tired of six-week lead times and 10,000-unit minimums from traditional print houses. We built the company we wished existed: fast, honest pricing, and equally happy to print 50 labels for a first-time candle maker or 500,000 for a national beverage brand.

Today we run three in-house presses out of our Austin, Texas facility, ship to all 50 states, and work with everyone from solo Etsy sellers to retail-shelf CPG brands.

Our Mission

Make premium, production-ready label printing accessible to businesses of every size — without minimums that shut out small brands.

Our Vision

To be the print partner every growing product brand outgrows their local print shop for — and never leaves.
