Finish Comparison

Matte, Gloss or Soft-Touch?

Matte

Soft, Non-Reflective

A premium, modern look with zero glare — the most popular choice for skincare and boutique packaging.

Gloss

Vibrant & Reflective

Makes colors pop under retail lighting — a strong choice for beverages and bold, colorful branding.

Soft-Touch

Velvety & Tactile

A suede-like texture customers feel before they read — reserved for premium, gift-ready products.
