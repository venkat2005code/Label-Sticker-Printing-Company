About Us

Label Printing Specialists Since 2014.

Printora Labels started in 2014 when our founder, a packaging designer, got tired of six-week lead times and 10,000-unit minimums from traditional print houses. Today we run three in-house presses out of our Austin, Texas facility and ship to all 50 states.

We work with everyone from solo Etsy sellers to retail-shelf CPG brands — and every order gets the same attention to quality.

  • Free digital proof — nothing prints until you approve it
  • Colour-managed from proof to final roll
  • Printed, die-cut on site and quality-checked before shipping

Printing & Finishing

Print and Finish Options for Every Label.

Pair our colour-managed digital printing with the finish that suits your product, shelf and budget.

Digital Printing

Vivid, colour-accurate CMYK printing on our in-house presses, from 50-unit test runs to full production.

Matte Finish

A soft, non-reflective finish with zero glare — the most popular choice for skincare and boutique packaging.

Gloss Finish

A vibrant, reflective finish that makes colours pop under retail lighting.

Lamination

Protective laminate layers — including waterproof and soft-touch — that guard against moisture, scuffs and handling.

UV Coating

Foil Finishing
